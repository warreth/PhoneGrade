## 6. Why the author asks for this

Two reasons, and neither is vanity.

The first is honesty. A shop owner deciding whether to grade a customer's phone should
be able to look inside the tool rather than take a promise on trust. Publishing the
source is what makes that possible, and it is only worth publishing if what comes out
is still traceable to the person who wrote it.

The second is that the name has a value that was built by hand. Anyone can read the
code. Only one person built the reputation that the name stands for, and the mark is
what keeps a customer's money from going to someone who has merely forked the source.
